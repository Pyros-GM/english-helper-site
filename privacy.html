Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · App: English Helper (com.eternaltgames.englishhelper) · Developer: Eternal-T Games

English Helper is an app for learning American English, made for Chinese speakers of all ages, including children. We collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account with us.
  • The app does not ask for your name, age, email, location, contacts, camera or photos. The microphone is used only if you choose speaking practice, and recordings never leave your device.
  • Your progress is kept on your device. If you use Google Play Games, a copy can also be kept in your own Google account.
  • We never sell or share personal information.

Information kept on your device

Your study progress (words learned, review schedule, scores, badges, study time) and your settings (language, voice, sound, theme and so on) are saved on your phone. They stay there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the app can sign in to it to back up your progress and settings ("cloud save") and to show leaderboards and achievements. This information is stored by Google in your Google Play Games account, not on our servers. You can play the whole app without signing in. Google's handling of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Purchases

Grade packs are bought through Google Play. Google handles all payment details; we never see your card or payment information. The app only receives confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock the grades and restore them on a new device.

Update check

When the app starts, it may download a small file (version.json) from GitHub Pages to see whether a newer version is on Google Play. No information about you is sent with this request. Like any website, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address in its server logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Voices

Pronunciation audio is recorded into the app. If a recording is missing, the app may use your phone's built-in text-to-speech, which runs on your device.

Speaking practice (microphone)

In Flashcards you can tap 🎤 to record yourself saying a word and compare it with the app's voice. The app asks for microphone permission the first time you do this. The recording is kept only in the phone's memory while it plays back. It is never saved or sent anywhere, and it is gone when you move to the next card. You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

Progress report

On the Stats screen you can share a short text summary of your progress (words mastered, study time, grades). It is sent only when you tap Share, and only to the app or person you choose.

Feedback and links

If you choose "Feedback" in Settings, your email app opens a message to us with the app version and phone model filled in. We only use emails to reply to you. The app also has links to Google Play and our Discord community; those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

English Helper is designed for learners of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no ads, no chat and no way to share information with other users. Sign-in to Google Play Games is optional and is managed by Google under its own policies for children's accounts. Parents or guardians who have questions, or who want any information deleted, can contact us at the address below.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything stored on your device. Cloud save data can be deleted from your Google Play Games settings. We do not hold any other copy of your data, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026年11月19日 · 应用：美语助手 English Helper（com.eternaltgames.englishhelper）· 开发者：Eternal-T Games

美语助手是一款学习美式英语的应用，面向各年龄段的中文使用者，包括儿童。我们尽量少收集信息。本政策说明应用如何处理信息。

简要说明

  • 没有广告、没有数据分析、没有追踪，也不需要在我们这里注册账号。
  • 应用不会索取你的姓名、年龄、邮箱、位置、通讯录、相机或照片。只有在你选择练习口语时才会使用麦克风，录音不会离开你的设备。
  • 你的学习进度保存在你的设备上。如果你使用 Google Play 游戏，也可以在你自己的 Google 账号中保存一份副本。
  • 我们绝不出售或分享个人信息。

保存在设备上的信息

你的学习进度（已学单词、复习计划、分数、徽章、学习时间）和设置（语言、语音、声音、主题等）保存在你的手机上，直到你清除应用数据或卸载应用。

Google Play 游戏（可选）

如果你在设备上使用 Google Play 游戏，应用可以登录它来备份你的进度和设置（"云存档"），并显示排行榜和成就。这些信息由 Google 保存在你的 Google Play 游戏账号中，而不是我们的服务器上。不登录也可以使用应用的全部内容。Google 对这些信息的处理适用 Google 隐私权政策。你可以在 Google Play 游戏应用或 Google 账号设置中管理或删除 Play 游戏数据。

购买

年级包通过 Google Play 购买。所有付款信息都由 Google 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡或付款信息。应用只会收到购买了什么的确认，用来解锁年级，并在新设备上恢复购买。

更新检查

应用启动时，可能会从 GitHub Pages 下载一个小文件（version.json），查看 Google Play 上是否有新版本。这个请求不会发送任何关于你的信息。和所有网站一样，GitHub 可能会在服务器日志中记录 IP 地址等技术信息，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。

语音

发音音频已内置在应用中。如果缺少某段录音，应用可能会使用手机自带的文字转语音功能，该功能在你的设备上运行。

口语练习（麦克风）

在单词卡中，你可以点 🎤 录下自己读单词的声音，并和应用的标准发音比较。第一次使用时，应用会请求麦克风权限。录音只在播放时暂存在手机内存中，不会被保存或发送到任何地方，切换到下一张卡片后就会消失。你可以随时在手机设置中关闭该权限。

学习报告

在统计页面，你可以分享一份简短的学习进度总结（已掌握的单词、学习时间、年级）。只有在你点“分享”时才会发送，而且只发送给你选择的应用或联系人。

反馈和链接

如果你在设置中选择"反馈"，你的邮件应用会打开一封写给我们的邮件，并自动填入应用版本和手机型号。我们只用邮件回复你。应用中还有指向 Google Play 和我们 Discord 社区的链接，这些网站有各自的隐私政策。

儿童

美语助手适合各年龄段的学习者，包括13岁以下的儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。应用没有广告、没有聊天，也无法与其他用户分享信息。登录 Google Play 游戏是可选的，并由 Google 按照其儿童账号政策管理。家长或监护人如有疑问，或希望删除任何信息，请通过下方地址联系我们。

删除你的信息

卸载应用或清除应用数据会删除保存在设备上的所有内容。云存档数据可以在 Google Play 游戏设置中删除。我们不保存你数据的任何其他副本，但欢迎你随时联系我们提出任何请求。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面和上方的生效日期。
